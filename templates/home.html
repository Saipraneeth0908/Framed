Frequently asked questions

What the current gallery experience supports today.

What can I customize?+

Every product supports a choice of frame, size, and poster theme. The displayed price updates with those selections.

How does the set discount work?+

The cart automatically applies 10% off when the total quantity reaches three or more posters.

Can I preview a poster in my room?+

Yes. Product pages include a local wall-preview tool. Your uploaded room photo stays in your browser and is not sent to the server.

Does checkout take payment?+

Not yet. Checkout is clearly marked as a demonstration and does not capture payment or create a persistent order.
